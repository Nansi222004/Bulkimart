## Deliberate absences

No dedicated marketplace PAYMENT_PENDING / PAYMENT_FAILED / PAYMENT_CANCELLED / PAYMENT_SUCCEEDED push constants are implemented. Payment state remains authoritative in Payment and order recovery; successful capture now triggers the eligible seller ORDER_CREATED event. No new exchange, subscription, general wallet-topup, courier-webhook or coin push event was invented. Coin constants without publishers do not imply working push delivery. Retired campaign/promotion constants are not re-enabled.
