S C O P E O F W O R K
Multi-Vendor E-Commerce & Quick Commerce Platform
1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive Multi-
Vendor E-Commerce & Quick Commerce Platform that enables
customers to purchase a wide range of products including
grocery, garments, household essentials, handicrafts, and locally
manufactured goods through a single application.
The platform will also feature a dedicated Quick Commerce
(Grocery) module with scheduled subscription deliveries, along
with a Self Help Group (SHG) Marketplace where SHG members
can sell homemade and community products.
A unique feature of the platform is the integration of the client's
CDS Community Membership System, allowing existing members
to verify and view their membership status directly within the
application.
The solution will support both General Vendors and SHG Vendors,
while also enabling small sellers to market products under the
client's own brand through a dedicated branding and packaging
approval process.
The complete ecosystem will consist of:
User Mobile Application (Android & iOS)
Seller Panel
Delivery Partner Application
Super Admin Web Panel
